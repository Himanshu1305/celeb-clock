# Article Content Template

Use this template when drafting a new SEO article (`/articles/[slug]`).

## Inputs
- **Target keyword:**
- **Search intent:** (informational / comparison / how-to)
- **Primary CTA:** (`/birthday-report` for astrology, `/longevity-calculator` for longevity)

## Structure

1. **H1** — includes the target keyword, ≤ 60 chars so the `| BornClock` title stays ≤ 70.
2. **Intro (40-60 words)** — answer the query in the first paragraph (AEO/featured-snippet friendly).
3. **Body sections (H2s)** — one H2 per sub-question. Use tables/lists where they help.
4. **Sourced claims** — cite WHO, GIA, Harvard, NIH, or Wikidata as appropriate.
5. **FAQ block** — 3-5 Q&As, wired to `FAQPage` JSON-LD.
6. **CTAs** — at least **2** contextual links to the primary CTA tool.
7. **Internal links** — 2-4 links to related articles / tools.

## Rules
- Minimum 800 words for pillar articles; 400+ for supporting pieces.
- Title ≤ 70 chars including `| BornClock`.
- One canonical, one OG image, `Article` + `FAQPage` schema.
- No thin content, no keyword stuffing, no unsourced medical claims.
