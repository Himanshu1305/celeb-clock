# Celebrity Profile Content Template

Use this template when expanding or drafting a celebrity birthday profile
(`/celebrity/[slug]/`). Keep it factual, sourced, and human — never AI-refusal
or filler language.

## Inputs
- **Name:**
- **Date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD):**
- **Known for:**
- **Category:** (Actor / Cricketer / Musician / Politician / …)

## Structure

1. **Opening (1-2 sentences)** — who they are and why they matter. Lead with the name.
2. **Career highlights** — 2-3 concrete achievements with dates.
3. **Birthday intelligence** — weave in their Western zodiac, Vedic rashi,
   nakshatra, and Life Path number (these render automatically; the prose should
   reference them naturally, e.g. "As a Scorpio with a Life Path 8, …").
4. **Cosmic twins** — mention that visitors sharing this birthday can compare via
   `/birthday-report` and `/compatibility`.
5. **Internal links** — link to the relevant `/celebrity/[hub]` and at least one
   `/born-on/[month]-[day]` page.

## Rules
- 130-180 words for the bio body.
- Mention the celebrity's name at least twice.
- No markdown headings inside the bio string; plain prose only.
- No "as an AI", no "I cannot", no bracketed placeholders.
- Every factual claim should be verifiable on Wikipedia/Wikidata.
