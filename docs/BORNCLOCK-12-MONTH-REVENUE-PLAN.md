# BornClock — 12-Month Revenue Plan
## Objective: ₹1 crore/month · Solo founder · Written 31 Jul 2026 · Revisit monthly

---

## 0. THE HONEST FRAME (read this first, every time)

**Base case at Month 12: ₹8–15L/month. Aggressive-but-earned: ₹25–40L. ₹1 Cr requires
an outlier event** — a viral moment, a B2B whale, or paid acquisition unlocking at scale.
Benchmarks: Astrotalk took ~4 years and a marketplace model to ₹1 Cr/mo (FY25: ₹1,214 Cr,
CAC ₹600–900, LTV ₹1,500). GaneshaSpeaks, a 20-year brand, does ~₹1.1 Cr/mo. Co-Star does
~₹3.3 Cr/mo with a viral US app. This plan maximises the probability of the outlier while
guaranteeing the compounding base. Decisions are made at gates, not on hope.

**The one structural truth:** ₹199 one-time reports cannot carry ₹1 Cr (needs 35K+
sales/mo ≈ 3M+ high-intent visits). The number is only reachable by stacking FOUR engines:
subscriptions (AI-Coach-led) + two report SKUs + B2B licensing + affiliate. Every month's
work must feed at least one engine.

## 2. THE FOUR PRODUCT MOVES (build once, feed all engines)

**P1 — Unified BornClock Coach (Month 1–2).** One AI assistant, every surface: chat with
your longevity results, your birthday chart, weekly rhythm/fitness check-in, family
planning. Free = 5 messages/month (taste); ₹299 tier = generous; new ₹499 / $9.99
"Coach+" = unlimited + 7-day forecasts + calendar export. This is THE subscription
product. Cost: Claude Code weeks, Anthropic API pennies vs ARPU.

**P2 — Longevity Blueprint, paid SKU #2 (Month 1).** ₹299 / $9.99 one-time: the science-
side sibling of Birthday Blueprint — bio-age, life-expectancy drivers, What-If scenarios,
personalised habit plan, printable. Self-purchase + "for Dad" gifting angle. Reuses the
entire report engine. Honesty framing mandatory (estimates, not diagnostics).

**P3 — Affiliate funnel (Month 1, days).** On free calculator results: "Estimated
biological age 43 — confirm with a real panel" → lab affiliate (India) / kit+wearable
affiliates (global). Honest copy only. Passive from day one.

**P4 — B2B package (Month 3).** One pitch deck + one white-label demo (their logo on the
assessment + report + coach). Pricing: ₹8–15L/yr per insurer/wellness-platform pilot, or
₹30–60/employee/yr corporate. Target list: HDFC Ergo, Niva Bupa, Star (wellness programs),
Cult/HealthifyMe-adjacent platforms, corporate gifting cos (birthday reports at scale).

## 3. TRAFFIC & DISTRIBUTION PLAN

### 3a. SEO (the compounding base — already 1,331 pages live)
- **Programmatic expansion to ~5,000 pages by M6:** 12 month hubs (done tonight), 366
  date pages enriched with national days (done tonight), zodiac hubs; ADD: 100 "born on
  the same day as {celebrity}" pages (map to existing 28K-celebrity DB), 365 "birthday
  compatibility {date}+{date}" top pairs, city-agnostic gift guides ("birthday gift for
  dad who has everything" class), fitness/rhythm cluster (6 done tonight → 25 by M4),
  longevity cluster ("life expectancy calculator by country/lifestyle", "biological age
  vs real age", What-If scenario pages ×25 factors).
- **AEO/GEO:** every page keeps the direct-answer paragraph + FAQPage schema pattern;
  llms.txt; target being the cited answer for "biorhythm calculator", "life expectancy
  calculator India", "born on {date}".
- **Authority (the missing input):** DR is the constraint, not content. Monthly: 2 digital-
  PR data stories from own data ("most common birthday in India", "which birth month
  lives longest — 57-country data"), HARO/Qwoted responses, 10 niche directory/tool
  listings, guest posts on wellness/parenting blogs. Target DR 30+ by M6, 40+ by M12.
- **Targets:** M3 30K organic visits/mo · M6 100K · M9 250K · M12 500K. (AstroSage-class
  is millions; 500K is aggressive-realistic for a 1-yr-old domain executing well.)

### 3b. Launches (the initial spikes — Month 1–2)
- **Product Hunt:** launch "BornClock — the timing layer for your life" (lead with the
  free tools + Coach, not the ₹199 report). Prep: 2 weeks of engaging genuinely, teaser
  page, 20 supporter DMs, launch Tue/Wed 12:01am PT, founder present all day. Goal: top-5
  of day → backlinks + first USD subs.
- **Others, one per week:** Hacker News Show HN (lead with the data/engineering story —
  28K celebrities, 366 programmatic pages, solo+AI build story does well there),
  r/InternetIsBeautiful (free calculator angle), r/SideProject + r/indiehackers (build
  story), BetaList, Uneed, MicroLaunch, There's An AI For That, Futurepedia (AI Coach),
  DevHunt. India: YourStory/Inc42 pitch ("solo founder + AI built a GST-compliant SaaS
  in X weeks" is a story they run).
- **AppSumo-class LTD (Month 4–5, decision gate):** a lifetime-deal spike can do
  ₹8–30L in a month and 5–10K users for the flywheel — at the cost of discounting.
  Run ONLY if subscriptions retain well by then (else you sell lifetime access to a
  product people wouldn't have renewed anyway — fine; if retention is great, skip).

### 3c. Google Ads (paid — gated, not assumed)
- **The unit-economics law:** never spend where CAC > 1/3 of 12-month LTV.
  ₹199 report: max viable CAC ≈ ₹60–70 → only ultra-cheap intent works.
  ₹299 sub (assume 4-mo avg life initially → LTV ~₹1,200): max CAC ₹350–400.
  $9.99 Coach+ (LTV ~$40): max CAC ~$12 — this is where ads can actually scale.
- **Month 2–3 test budget: ₹30–50K/mo total.** Campaigns: (1) Search, exact/phrase,
  high-intent only — "birthday gift for {relation}", "life expectancy calculator",
  "biological age test free", "kundli alternative english"; (2) global Search USD —
  "birthday book gift", "personalized birthday gift last minute" (instant delivery is
  the ad angle vs $95 Birthdate Co); (3) remarketing to free-tool users (cheapest
  conversions you'll ever buy). NO broad display, NO video initially.
- **Scale rule:** any ad set with CAC under the cap for 2 consecutive weeks → +50%
  budget; two weeks over cap → kill. By M6 either ads are a scaling engine (₹2–5L/mo
  spend profitably) or they're off and the money goes to digital PR.
- **Compliance note:** health-adjacent ad copy stays claim-free ("estimate", "explore")
  — Google health policy + our own honesty rule.

### 3d. Social & viral (solo-sustainable version only)
- The product's built-in loop IS the strategy: every gifted report exposes a recipient →
  strengthen with "Create yours" on shared reports, WhatsApp-first share cards, and a
  referral give-get (1 free report credit both sides) in Month 3.
- Content: 3 short-form pieces/week MAX (reels/shorts: "your age on Mars", "born the
  same day as", rhythm check-ins) — batch-produced monthly with Claude Code scripts.
  X/LinkedIn: build-in-public thread weekly (feeds PH/HN/press flywheel).
- Explicitly NOT doing (solo reality): daily posting, community management, influencer
  outreach at scale. One micro-influencer barter test in M4 (free Coach+ for a reel).

### 3e. Email & retention (the multiplier on everything above)
- Weekly digest live (flip after review) → the habit spine.
- Occasion engine: birthday-of-a-loved-one reminders ("Priya's birthday in 10 days —
  gift her Blueprint?") — the highest-intent email a gifting product can send. M2.
- Winback (day 21 post-report), credit-expiry nudges, Coach re-engagement. All
  server-side send-once discipline as established.

- Time: ~10 focused hrs/week post-Aug 1. Split: 4 product (Claude Code sessions), 3
  growth (launches/PR/ads), 2 B2B, 1 metrics/review. Anything not on this plan = no.

  already in place). Buy, don't build: ads management stays manual-simple; no CRM until
  2 B2B pilots; no hires until ₹5L/mo sustained (then a VA for support/content ops).
