Before you begin

A few good questions.

Is this a medical tool?

No. These mockups are informational interface demonstrations. They are not medical devices, diagnostic tools or sources of treatment advice.

Why is biological age shown as not measured?

No biomarkers have been supplied, and no biological-age model is connected. A date of birth provides chronological age, not a measured biological age.

Does changing the form calculate my life expectancy?

No. Your chronological age and context update locally. The 78.4-year figure remains clearly marked as synthetic design data. A production estimate requires an identified, validated model and source dataset.

Do the habit sliders change my predicted lifespan?

No. They demonstrate an interaction only. The page does not invent extra years of life from sleep or activity entries.
